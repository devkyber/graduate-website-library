100 graduate-student websites ranked for design inspiration
Editorial ranking · 29 October 2026
All 2,281 sites visually screened; 152 finalists compared; 100 fresh desktop screenshots.
Ranking weighs composition, typography, visual coherence, distinctiveness and usefulness as inspiration. Institution prestige is not a ranking factor. This is an editorial judgment, not an objective score or a full mobile/accessibility audit.

01. Sejal Agarwal — Waterloo
Website: https://sejalagarwal.ca/
Website source repository: https://github.com/sejal-agarwal/sejal-notebook-portfolio
Style: Colorful
Screenshot: screenshots/001-512.jpg
Why it works:
  - The dotted notebook background, pastel navigation tabs, taped portrait and handwritten details belong to one consistent visual language.
  - A large, dark headline and two clear buttons keep the playful decoration subordinate to the introduction.
Borrow this: Choose one physical metaphor, such as a notebook, and repeat it in a few carefully controlled details.
